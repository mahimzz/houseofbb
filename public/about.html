Our story

Inspired by the Sultan's Pavilion.

A UK label, inspired by Turkey.

House of BB was inspired by the Hünkâr Kasrı, the Sultan's Pavilion of the New Mosque in Eminönü, Istanbul. Set above a grand archway beside the Spice Bazaar, it was once a private retreat for sultans and queen mothers.

Inside, almost every wall is covered in 17th-century Iznik tiles: cobalt and turquoise tulips, carnations and saz leaves, repeating in patterns that seem to move as you walk through the rooms.

We wanted to carry that feeling with us. Our first drop, À la Turca, takes the spirit of those tiles and places it on a premium white tee, limited to 50 pieces.
