Our story

It started with a wall of tiles.

A UK label, inspired by Turkey.

In [YEAR], [FOUNDER NAME] spent an afternoon in Istanbul that changed everything. Inside a small mosque near the Spice Bazaar, every wall was covered in hand-painted Iznik tiles: cobalt flowers, turquoise leaves and patterns repeating as far as the eye could see.

The sketchbook came out that day and never really went away. Back home in [CITY], those pages of tulips, carnations and saz leaves became an idea: what if you could wear that feeling?

House of BB was born from that idea. The two Bs stand for [WHAT BB STANDS FOR]. Our first piece, À la Turca, takes those tiles from the walls of Istanbul and places them on a premium white tee.
