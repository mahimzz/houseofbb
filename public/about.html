Our story

Inspired by Topkapı Palace.

A UK label, inspired by Turkey.

House of BB was inspired by Topkapı Palace in Istanbul, home of the Ottoman sultans for nearly four centuries. High above the Bosphorus, its courtyards and private rooms hold some of the finest Iznik tilework ever made.

Walls of cobalt and turquoise tulips, carnations and saz leaves repeat from floor to ceiling, in patterns that seem to move as you walk through the palace. The design on our first tee is taken from those tiles.

We wanted to carry that feeling with us. Our first drop, À la Turca, places that tilework on a premium white tee, limited to 50 pieces.
